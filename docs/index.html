Universe. Operating-company IPOs as defined by Jay Ritter. Count charts follow Table 3b (US issuers, offer ≥ $5, NYSE + Nasdaq main boards; excludes ADRs, units, SPACs, closed-end funds, REITs, LPs, banks/S&Ls). Market-cap charts follow Table 1a (aggregate value at first-day close, broader Table 1 sample).

Quarterly split. Ritter reports annual figures. From 2015 the annual totals are split using the quarterly shape of the filtered Nasdaq calendar; earlier years use seasonal weights with event overrides.

Years after Ritter's latest. Nasdaq IPO calendar priced deals filtered to a Table 3b proxy (NYSE, Nasdaq Global/Global Select; offer ≥ $5; no SPACs, units, funds, trusts, REITs). Counts × 0.665 (pooled Ritter ÷ calendar, 2015–2025; yearly range 0.50–0.70). Base market cap = base proceeds × 6.49 (pooled; yearly range 4.2–10.6, so treat quarterly market caps as approximate).

Mega-IPOs. Separate layer at first-day-close market cap. Listed deals are detected automatically from Nasdaq quote data once their ticker trades; expected deals use the midpoint of reported valuation ranges, with no first-day pop, and roll forward a quarter at a time if they slip.

Current quarter. Quarter-to-date calendar actuals plus the forecast pro-rated for the days remaining.

S&P 500 denominator. To 2025: approximate year-end totals held within each year. From 2026: SPY close × anchor (S&P 500 cap $68.45T on 2026-09-30); forecast quarters use the latest value.

Exclusions. SKHY: US cross-listing of an already-listed Korean issuer (ADR), Jul 2026; outside Ritter's operating-company IPO universe
